Games for Little Kids

Free browser games for ages 4–6. No ads, no sign-up, works on phones and tablets.

  • Word Train Tap the word that matches the picture to speed the train to the next station.
  • ABC Balloon Pop Tie letter balloons to the basket in order and watch it fly away.
  • Ball Machine A drag-and-drop physics puzzle: help the ball into the bucket.
  • Space Explorer A gentle top-down space puzzle.
